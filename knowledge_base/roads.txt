# Roads & Pavement Infrastructure: Citizen Filing Guide

Department In-Charge:
Roads Department / Highways & Minor Ports Department / Municipal Engineering Works

Common Grievance Types:
1. Hazardous potholes and cratered road surfaces causing accidents or traffic congestion.
2. Incomplete road construction, abandoned asphalt or trenching work by cable/pipe contractors.
3. Broken pavements, footpaths, curb stones, or missing cover slabs on roadside ditches.
4. Waterlogging on roads due to lack of gradient or storm water inlets.
5. Missing or damaged street signages, unpainted speed breakers, and road median breaks.

Information Essential for Quick Resolution:
- Exact Location / Landmark: E.g., "Near Katpadi Railway Station roundtana, on Main Road towards Bagayam".
- Size & Hazard Level: Deep pothole (depth > 6 inches) causing two-wheelers to slip or heavy vehicle accidents.
- Duration: Persisting for multiple weeks or worsened after recent monsoon rains.
- Affected Traffic: State whether it is on an arterial bus route, commercial corridor, or residential street.

Expected Turnaround Time (TAT):
- Hazardous potholes on main roads: Patchwork within 48 to 72 hours.
- Major resurfacing / trench restoration: 15 to 30 days depending on tender/contractor cycle.
