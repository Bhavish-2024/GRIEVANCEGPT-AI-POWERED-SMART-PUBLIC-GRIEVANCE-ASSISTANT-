# Public Health & Epidemic Prevention: Citizen Filing Guide

Department In-Charge:
Public Health Department / City Municipal Health Office

Common Grievance Types:
1. Mosquito breeding hotspots, stagnant pools of water, necessity of fogging operations.
2. Contaminated drinking water outbreaks leading to gastrointestinal illness in neighborhood.
3. Unhygienic food establishments, street food vendors operating in unsanitary conditions.
4. Stray dog menace, aggressive packs of dogs, urgent requirement of birth control (ABC) and anti-rabies vaccination.
5. Inadequate sanitation facilities in primary health centres (PHC) or government clinics.

Information Essential for Quick Resolution:
- Specific Area: Locality, water bodies, or vacant plots where breeding/issues are concentrated.
- Number of Affected Persons: If neighbors or children are suffering from dengue/malaria/cholera symptoms.
- Description of Health Risk: Presence of larvae, stagnant sewage, or uninspected open drainage.

Expected Turnaround Time (TAT):
- Disease outbreak report / emergency fogging: Within 24 hours.
- Stray dog vaccination / catching: 3 to 5 working days.
- PHC complaint: 48 to 72 hours.
