# Sanitation & Solid Waste Management: Citizen Filing Guide

Department In-Charge:
Sanitation Department / Public Health & Conservancy Division

Common Grievance Types:
1. Garbage not collected from residential doorsteps for multiple days.
2. Overflowing public community bins / dumper placers scattering waste on roads.
3. Dead animals lying on public roads requiring immediate removal.
4. Open dumping and burning of plastic/municipal solid waste causing toxic air pollution.
5. Inadequate street sweeping in commercial or residential areas.

Information Essential for Quick Resolution:
- Exact Spot / Dustbin Location: Landmark (e.g., "Corner of 3rd Cross Street, near Govt High School").
- Duration of Accumulation: E.g., "Garbage lying uncollected for 3 consecutive days".
- Environmental & Health Hazard: Rotten odor, stray dogs/rodents gathering, proximity to food stalls or schools.

Expected Turnaround Time (TAT):
- Overflowing bin clearance: Within 24 hours.
- Dead animal removal: Within 12 hours (Emergency service).
- Regular door-to-door collection resumption: 24 to 48 hours.
