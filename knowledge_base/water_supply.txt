# Water Supply Issues: Citizen Filing Guide

Department In-Charge:
Water Supply Department / Municipal Water Works Board

Common Grievance Types:
1. Complete disruption of drinking water supply (No water supply for several days).
2. Contaminated or discolored water (Muddy, foul smell, sewage mixing in drinking water).
3. Low water pressure / inadequate supply duration.
4. Main water supply pipeline burst or leakage on public roads.
5. Delayed or missing municipal water tankers.
6. Defective or disputed water meters and excessive billing.

Information Essential for Quick Resolution:
- Locality / Ward / Street Name: E.g., "5th Cross Street, Gandhi Nagar, Katpadi, Ward 14".
- Duration of Failure: Exact number of days or hours (e.g., "Water not received for 4 consecutive days since Friday").
- Extent of Problem: Does it affect only one residence (likely internal line blockage) or the entire street/neighborhood (main distribution line fault)?
- Pumping Schedule: If supply was previously on alternate days or specific hours.
- Health Risk: If water is discolored or smells like drainage, mention "Urgent Public Health Hazard".

Expected Turnaround Time (TAT):
- Pipeline burst / contamination: Within 24 hours.
- Complete disruption: 24 to 48 hours (temporary water tankers dispatched in interim).
- Pressure / schedule adjustments: 3 to 5 working days.
