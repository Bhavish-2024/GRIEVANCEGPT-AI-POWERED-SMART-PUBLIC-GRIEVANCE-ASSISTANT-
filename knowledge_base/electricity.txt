# Electricity & Street Lighting: Citizen Filing Guide

Department In-Charge:
Electricity Department (Discom / TNEB) / Municipal Electrical Engineering

Common Grievance Types:
1. Frequent unannounced power cuts or prolonged outage in locality.
2. Low voltage causing damage to domestic home appliances or water motors.
3. Dark streets due to fused, broken, or continuously unlit street light fixtures.
4. Dangling or snapped low-hanging high-voltage electrical wires posing immediate life hazard.
5. Sparking electrical transformers or tilted junction boxes.

Information Essential for Quick Resolution:
- Exact Pole Number / Location: Street name, pole number stencil (e.g., "Pole #T42, Gandhi Road").
- Timing & Pattern: Does the outage happen at night, intermittently, or total blackout?
- Immediate Hazard: If live wires touch ground, mention "Immediate Life Threat / High Hazard".
- Consumer Service Number: If specific to an electricity meter.

Expected Turnaround Time (TAT):
- Hazardous hanging wire / transformer sparking: 2 to 4 hours (Emergency Priority).
- Street light repairs: 48 to 72 hours.
- Voltage fluctuation resolution: 3 to 5 working days.
